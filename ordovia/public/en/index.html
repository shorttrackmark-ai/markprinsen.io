Frequently asked questions

Do I have to install anything?

No. Ordovia runs in your browser, also on a phone.

Can I look around without signing up?

Yes: “Try the example” opens a small company with working processes. You can do anything in it; it is reset every night.

What happens after the trial?

Without a subscription your workspace becomes read-only: you can still view and download everything, but not change anything. If you choose a plan, everything continues right where it was.

Where is my data?

In the European Union, in a separate database per company. Read more in the privacy statement and the data processing agreement.

Does it work with Microsoft 365?

Yes. Processes can be started and followed from Power Automate, and notifications can go to Teams. Nothing to install.
