Privacy statement

Draft 1 · 16 November 2026

1. Who is responsible

[company name], [address], KvK [number] (“Ordovia”) is the controller of the personal data we need to manage accounts, subscriptions and the website. For the data a company puts into Ordovia itself (forms, cases, documents) Ordovia is the processor and the company is the controller; see the data processing agreement. Questions: [e-mail address].

2. Which data

  • Account: name, e-mail address, language choice and your password (only as an irreversible fingerprint, never readable).
  • Company and subscription: company name, chosen plan, billing details (processed by Stripe; we do not see card or bank details).
  • Use in processes: who did which step and when, as the record of the process. Ordovia does not record what you look at, how long you type, or what you do outside Ordovia. Everything recorded about you is shown on the “What Ordovia knows about you” page in the app, and can be downloaded.
  • Security and faults: counters of failed sign-in attempts (per e-mail address and IP address, deleted after 24 hours) and error reports without content (deleted after 14 days).

3. Why, and on what basis

PurposeBasis
Providing an account and the service, sending invitations and password mailsPerformance of the contract
Subscriptions, invoices, VATContract and legal obligation (bookkeeping)
Security, preventing abuse, finding faultsLegitimate interest

We do not sell data, show advertising, or follow you with analytics or advertising cookies.

4. Cookies and browser storage

Ordovia only uses functional storage: a session cookie (staying signed in), a cookie saying which company you belong to, and your language choice. No consent is needed for that. There are no tracking or advertising cookies.

5. Who we share data with

  • Cloudflare – hosting and storage; companies’ data is stored in the European Union.
  • Stripe – payments (only when a subscription is taken out).
  • E-mail provider [to be chosen] – sending invitation and password mails (name, e-mail address, link).

Nobody else receives data, unless the law requires it. If data is processed outside the EU, that only happens with appropriate safeguards (such as standard contractual clauses).

6. How long we keep data

  • Account data: as long as the account exists; a company can delete everything permanently itself (Settings).
  • Backups: 30 days.
  • Invoice data: 7 years (tax retention obligation).
  • Sign-in counters: 24 hours. Error reports: 14 days.

7. Your rights

You may see your data (the “What Ordovia knows about you” page), have it corrected or deleted, restrict processing, object, and take your data with you (download). For data your company puts into Ordovia, ask the administrator of your company first. Otherwise e-mail [e-mail address]; we respond within a month. You may also complain to the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).

8. Security

Each company’s data is kept separately in its own database. Connections are encrypted, passwords are stored encrypted, sign-in is protected against repeated guessing, and administrator actions are logged. More in annex II of the data processing agreement.

9. Changes

We may change this statement; the latest version is always here.
