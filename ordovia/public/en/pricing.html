Pricing questions

How are people counted?

The number of active people in your company. You can see it in the app under Plan & billing, with this month’s usage. If you go over your plan’s limit, you choose a larger plan.

What does the trial cost?

Nothing. 14 days of full use, no credit card. After that your workspace becomes read-only until you choose a plan.

Can I cancel?

Yes, at any time from the payment details page. You can always download your data.

Do I get an invoice with VAT?

Yes. VAT is calculated at checkout and you get invoices through the payment page.
