Data processing agreement

Draft 1 · 16 November 2026 · part of the terms of service

Parties: the Customer (the “Controller”) and [company name], trading as Ordovia (the “Processor”). This agreement applies automatically once the Customer uses the Service, and for as long as the Processor processes the Customer’s personal data.

1. Subject

The Processor processes personal data on behalf of the Controller only to provide the Service, as described in annex I, and on the Controller’s documented instructions (the agreement, this agreement and the use of the Service). If, in the Processor’s opinion, an instruction infringes the law, the Processor says so.

2. Confidentiality

The Processor ensures that everybody with access to the personal data is bound by confidentiality.

3. Security

The Processor takes appropriate technical and organisational measures, as in annex II, and adjusts them as the state of the art and the risk require.

4. Sub-processors

The Controller gives general authorisation for the sub-processors in annex III. The Processor announces intended changes at least 30 days in advance (in the app or by e-mail); within that period the Controller may object with reasons. The Processor imposes the same obligations on sub-processors and remains responsible for them.

5. Data subject rights

The Processor helps the Controller by appropriate means with requests from data subjects. The Customer can view, correct, export and delete data itself through the Service. If the Processor receives a request directly from a data subject, it refers them to the Controller.

6. Personal data breaches

The Processor notifies the Controller of a breach of the security of personal data without undue delay and at the latest within [48] hours of discovery, with the information reasonably available.

7. Storage location and transfers

The Controller’s data is stored in the European Union. Transfers outside the EU only take place where needed for a sub-processor’s service and then with appropriate safeguards (such as standard contractual clauses).

8. Audit

On request the Processor provides the information needed to demonstrate compliance and cooperates with an audit, at most once a year, with reasonable notice and at the Controller’s cost unless the audit shows a shortcoming.

9. End of processing

The Controller can at any time export all data and have it permanently deleted through the Service. After the agreement ends, the Processor deletes the personal data at the latest [90] days after the subscription ends (backups at the latest 30 days after that), unless the law requires retention.

10. Liability and law

The terms of service apply to liability. Dutch law applies to this agreement.

Annex II – Security measures

  • Separation: each company has its own database and its own part of file storage; a session only works inside its own company.
  • Encryption: connections over HTTPS (HSTS); passwords only as a salted, hashed fingerprint (PBKDF2); sign-in and API tokens stored only as fingerprints.
  • Access: roles (user/administrator); 14-day sessions with secure cookies; signing out on all devices after a password reset; limits on sign-in attempts; invitation and password-reset links are single-use and time-limited.
  • Web security: strict Content-Security-Policy, protection against cross-site requests, no inline scripts, no framing.
  • Logging: administrator actions and account events are recorded; ordinary sign-ins and reading behaviour are not.
  • Backups and recovery: daily backup per company, kept 30 days, in the EU; restore by the administrator.
  • Other: data export at any time; error reports without content; automated tests of the separation between companies on every change.
